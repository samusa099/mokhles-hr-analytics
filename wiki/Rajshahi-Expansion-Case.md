# Rajshahi Expansion Case

## Scenario

Mokhles Group copied its mature Dhaka HR operating model into a new Rajshahi office. By Q4 FY2025, hiring, turnover, training, performance, leave and safety indicators were sending conflicting signals.

The participant must determine whether Rajshahi is:

- a failing location;
- a temporarily immature operation;
- or a location using the wrong workforce operating model.

## Decision requirement

Choose exactly one final decision:

- **Continue**
- **Pause**
- **Redesign**

Then recommend exactly three priority actions.

## Evidence boundary

- Use existing project data only.
- Do not add external market, salary or labour data.
- Do not invent missing facts.
- Label every important statement by evidence strength.

## Required evidence classes

| Classification | Use |
|---|---|
| Confirmed finding | Directly calculated from project data |
| Supported inference | Interpretation supported by multiple findings |
| Unproven hypothesis | Plausible explanation requiring more evidence |
| Unsupported claim | Claim that must not be used in the decision |

## Recommended analysis sequence

1. Compare Dhaka and Rajshahi workforce size and growth.
2. Examine hiring volume, time-to-fill and recruitment-source performance.
3. Separate voluntary and involuntary exits.
4. Compare early-tenure and established-employee turnover.
5. Review training participation and assessment movement.
6. Compare performance distribution and promotion readiness.
7. Check leave concentration and absence patterns.
8. Review safety incidents and corrective actions.
9. Test whether problems are broad or concentrated in specific departments or cohorts.
10. Build the decision from confirmed findings, not one headline KPI.

## Suggested final structure

1. Executive decision
2. Three strongest confirmed findings
3. Evidence by workforce domain
4. Alternative explanations
5. Decision risks and limitations
6. Exactly three priority actions
7. Monitoring metrics and review date
